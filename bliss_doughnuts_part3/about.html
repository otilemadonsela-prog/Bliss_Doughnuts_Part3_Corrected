Our story

Where Bliss began

A small doughnut idea grew from a love of sharing sweet treats with family, friends and the local community.

How it started

Bliss Doughnuts was started in 2021 in Springs, Johannesburg, South Africa, by two friends who believed that a doughnut could make an ordinary day feel special. What began as a small home-based business making treats for friends and family grew as more people discovered the doughnuts.

Meet the founders

The founders are the heart and hands behind the brand. Their idea came from seeing a gap in the market and believing that sweet treats can bring people together.

Our vision

To become a loved doughnut brand in Johannesburg, known for creativity, quality and excellent service while reaching nearby communities.

Our mission

To spread happiness one doughnut at a time by creating fresh, fun and flavourful treats and offering thoughtful customer service.
